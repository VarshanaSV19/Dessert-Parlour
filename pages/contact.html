How early should I place a custom cake order?

Please place custom cake orders at least 48 hours in advance. Larger celebration orders may require more time.

Do you provide dessert delivery?

Yes. Delivery is available within selected areas of Bangalore. Charges depend on distance and order size.

Are vegan and dairy-free desserts available?

Yes. We offer selected vegan and dairy-free items. Please mention your preference while placing the order.

Can I order dessert boxes for office events?

Yes. We prepare customised dessert boxes for office events, birthdays and larger celebrations.

Can I reserve seating for a celebration?

Small group reservations are available. Contact our team with the date, time and number of guests.

How should I inform you about an allergy?

Inform our team before ordering. Our products are prepared in a shared kitchen that handles dairy, nuts, gluten and other allergens.
