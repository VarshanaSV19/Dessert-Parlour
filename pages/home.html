Our Sweet Story

Born from a love for unforgettable flavours

Melt & Swirl began with one simple idea: ice cream should feel as special as the moments shared around it. What started as a small neighbourhood parlour has grown into a place where creativity and community come together.

We continue to prepare our desserts in small batches, pairing familiar favourites with exciting seasonal flavours.
